# Notes: Success Secrets of Solomon
## Full Book Reference for Ask Solomon App

---

## OVERVIEW & PURPOSE

Success Secrets of Solomon is a devotional/wisdom book built around the Book of Proverbs, written primarily as a father's teaching to his son. It is designed to be read ONE PAGE PER DAY with deep reflection. The author states: 'This book is for enlightenment, not entertainment.'

Solomon asked God for wisdom (not wealth or power), received it, and as a result received everything else - wealth, peace, honor, and success unrivaled in history.

**Core thesis:** Wisdom is the principal thing. Seek it first and everything else follows. Ignore it and self-induced pain, failure, and foolishness follow.

---

## TONE & VOICE OF THE BOOK

- Warm but direct - Like a loving father speaking to a son
- Practical and applicable - Every principle ties to real daily life
- Spiritually grounded - God is the source of all wisdom
- Contrasting - Constantly compares wise person vs. fool
- Encouraging but honest - Does not sugarcoat consequences of foolishness
- Personal and reflective - Author uses 'I' and 'we' freely
- Non-preachy but principled - Teaches divine law without being condemning
- Hopeful - Always points toward the better path; 'it is not too late'

**Key phrases the book uses:**
- 'Wisdom is the principal thing'
- 'Seek wisdom like treasure'
- 'The wise person...' vs. 'The fool...'
- 'There are two sides to life'
- 'Cause and effect / Sowing and reaping'
- 'Living by design, not default'
- 'Production Quadrant vs. Pleasure Quadrant'
- 'Guard your heart / Keep thy heart with all diligence'
- 'Fear of the Lord' = reverence and respect, not terror

## PART 1: FOUNDATIONS OF WISDOM (Proverbs 1-3)

### What Wisdom Is
- Wisdom is knowing HOW to use knowledge, not just possessing it
- Wisdom = divine intelligence + discretion + understanding
- It must be DESIRED, SOUGHT, and VALUED - it does not come passively
- 'Wisdom is secret not because it is hidden, but because it must be desired'
- Wisdom cries out publicly - those who do not hear it simply are not looking

### How to Get Wisdom
- Fear of the Lord (reverence, humility before God) is the BEGINNING of wisdom - Prov 1:7, 9:10
- Be teachable - open heart, receptive spirit
- Listen on purpose with intent to learn - Prov 1:5
- Seek it like treasure - passionate, persistent pursuit - Prov 2:1-5
- Pray for it - 'cry after it' - James 1:5 referenced
- Apply it - not just intellectual knowledge but lived-out discipline

### Who Receives Wisdom
- The humble, the reverent, the teachable
- Those who ask for it sincerely
- Those who pursue it passionately
- Those who 'incline their ear' and 'apply their heart'

### Who Does NOT Receive Wisdom
- Fools who 'despise wisdom and instruction' - Prov 1:7
- The arrogant/conceited (EGO = Edging God Out)
- The hard-headed and closed-minded
- Those who refuse to change

## PART 2: THE WISE PERSON vs. THE FOOL

### Characteristics of the WISE Person
- Reverent and humble toward God
- Teachable and open-minded
- Listens with intent to learn
- Seeks wise counsel
- Makes decisions based on principles, not emotion
- Guards their heart/mind diligently
- Speaks with purpose and precision
- Chooses relationships carefully
- Lives by design, not default
- Gives generously; honors God with substance
- Diligent and self-disciplined
- Has a long-term vision
- Receives correction positively
- Walks safely; has peace of mind; sleeps well
- Their life: peaceful, prosperous, honorable, pleasant, happy

### Characteristics of the FOOL
- Despises wisdom and instruction
- Hard-headed, closed-minded, unteachable
- Makes decisions based on emotion and ego
- Speaks without thinking; mouth pours out foolishness
- Lives for immediate pleasure
- Blames others for self-created problems
- Cannot see cause and effect in their own life
- Makes enemies of the wise; befriends the foolish
- Life is chaotic, dramatic, full of self-induced pain
- Never learns from pain - repeats mistakes
- Has passive desires (daydreams without doing)
- Wastes time, resources, opportunities
- Lives in fear and anxiety

## PART 3: THE SLUGGARD & THE SLOTHFUL (Proverbs 6, 20, 24, 26)

### The SLUGGARD
- Habitually makes excuses (e.g., 'there is a lion in the street')
- Always tired; addicted to sleep; drains energy through inactivity
- Passive desires - wants things but will not work for them
- Conceited - thinks he knows more than seven wise men
- Irritating to employers; requires constant management
- The ANT is the opposite model

### The SLOTHFUL
- Wastes what they have - does not appreciate blessings
- Gives minimum effort; neglects important things
- Makes life harder through procrastination and neglect
- Habitually overwhelmed by self-created messes
- Field overrun with thorns = consequence of neglect - Prov 24:30-34
- Creates poverty through attitude and neglect

### The ANT - Positive Model (Prov 6:6-11)
Three lessons from the ant:
1. INDUSTRIOUS - productive, active with purpose, nonstop in season
2. INITIATIVE - no guide or manager needed; self-motivated; proactive
3. INVESTING - stores food in summer for winter; future-focused; does more than minimum

### The DILIGENT Person
- Constant, earnest, persistent effort
- Pays painstaking attention to tasks
- Gives maximum energy; takes responsibility personally
- Will be recognized and promoted - 'shall stand before kings' - Prov 22:29
- Diligence = increase; slacking = decrease - Prov 10:4
- Monitors what they manage - 'know the state of thy flocks' - Prov 27:23
- Desire + diligence = prosperity; desire - diligence = delusion

## PART 4: MANAGING THE MIND (Proverbs 4:23, 23:7)

Key Verse: Proverbs 4:23
'Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life.'

### Core Principles
- We CAN manage our minds - God gave us this dominion
- We MUST manage our minds - if we do not, others will plant seeds in us
- Our outer life is a direct reflection of our inner thoughts
- 'As he thinketh in his heart, so is he' - Prov 23:7
- Thoughts are never neutral - always creating consequences
- We become what we think about most of the time

### The Garden Metaphor
- The mind is like a garden - left unattended, weeds (negative thoughts) grow fast
- Positive things require purposeful cultivation
- Others will plant seeds in our unguarded minds (media, culture, toxic people)

### 'Keep' = Multiple Meanings
1. GUARD - constant vigilance against outside attacks/influences
2. INSPECT - search within for harmful beliefs already planted
3. CULTIVATE - actively grow what you want within

### How to Manage the Mind - Practical Steps
1. Become self-aware - notice what you are thinking; 'hear' yourself think
2. Think on purpose - decide what fruit you want to grow; eliminate contrary thoughts
3. Focus as fertilizer - whatever you focus on grows; focus on positives
4. Personalize beliefs - 'God answers prayers' becomes 'God is answering MY prayers'
5. Meditate on God's Word - like David in Psalm 1; meditation becomes manifestation
6. Live by DESIGN not default - pre-decide who you will become; architect your life

### Three Effects of Thinking
- As we think, we BEHAVE - thoughts produce behaviors
- As we think, we BELIEVE - faith is a product of thinking
- As we think, we BECOME - transformation is caused by renewing the mind (Romans 12:2)

### Design Framework
1. Design Concept - big picture of who/what you want to be (write it down)
2. Design the Details - break into compartments: personal, professional, ministry, fitness
3. Develop the Design - daily steps toward the concept; God is Senior Partner

## PART 5: MANAGING THE MOUTH

### Core Principle: Words Have Power
- 'Death and life are in the power of the tongue' - Prov 18:21
- Words stimulate emotion - positive words stimulate positive emotions
- Words create fruit - we eat the fruit of our own words
- Words can wound like a sword OR produce health - Prov 12:18
- 'A wholesome tongue is a tree of life' - Prov 15:4

### Rules for Wise Communication
1. Avoid perverse/froward speech - no gossip, no useless chatter
2. Think before speaking - 'study to answer' - Prov 15:28
3. Soft answers turn away wrath - Law of Opposite Behavior - Prov 15:1
4. Spare your words - wise people talk less and say more - Prov 17:27
5. Refine words like silver - pure motive + right method - Prov 10:20
6. Right thing, right time, right way - the formula for constructive communication
7. Never hasty with words - haste leads to mistakes - Prov 29:20

### The Constructive Communicator
- Thinks like the listener, not themselves
- Makes knowledge appealing ('sweet') to the hearer - Prov 16:21
- Speaks to the individual heart; customizes communication
- Outcome-oriented, not output-oriented
- 'Pleasant words are as a honeycomb, sweet to the soul and health to the bones' - Prov 16:24

### The Destructive Communicator
- Pours out foolishness without filter
- Causes contention and conflict - Prov 18:6
- Gossips and whispers; destroys friendships - Prov 16:28
- 'A fool's mouth is his destruction' - Prov 18:7

### Talk vs. Action (Prov 14:23)
- Talk without labor = poverty
- Dreams become reality through WORK, not words alone
- Divide people into 'Talkers' and 'Doers'

## PART 6: VISION (Proverbs 29:18)

'Where there is no vision, the people perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he.'

### What Vision Is
- Seeing something not yet reality - a mental/spiritual concept born in the heart
- A divine process granted exclusively to humankind
- Vision = divine revelation of what CAN BE or SHOULD BE

### Why Vision Matters
- Without vision = stagnation to deterioration to perishing
- With vision = hope to positive expectation to positive action to transformation
- Vision activates the 'Alignment Principle' - right people, resources, opportunities align
- 'Where there is vision, there will always be provision'
- Vision focuses faith; faith without vision is dormant

### Characteristics of a True Vision
- Points the Way - gives direction
- Provides the Why - gives motivation and motive for action
- Plans the What - reveals beginning steps and ongoing action

### Steps of Vision Development - 4 Ss
1. SENSE IT - Internalization Stage: 'what if' and 'how can' thoughts
2. SEE IT - Conceptualization Stage: details form; becomes realistic in the mind
3. SAY IT - Manifestation Stage: share carefully (premature sharing invites doubt)
4. SEIZE IT - Materialization Stage: tangible daily steps; physical action begins

### Vision Principles
- Vision looks FORWARD, never backward - the past is dead; vision creates future
- Vision + Faith = unstoppable creative force
- People are limited by their vision or lack of it, not their circumstances
- To develop vision: write it, pray it, research it, daydream it, detail it

## PART 7: RELATIONSHIPS & FRIENDSHIPS

### Choosing Wise Relationships
- Relationships are either ASSETS or LIABILITIES
- 'Our relationships build or break us'
- Negative influences are great 'salespeople' - paint beautiful pictures without showing consequences
- Avoid those on destructive paths; follow those on wise paths
- We all follow something or someone - choose consciously

### True Friendship (Prov 17:17, 27:6, 27:9, 27:17)
- True friend = committed through adversity, like a brother
- 'Convenient friends' vs. 'Committed friends' - adversity reveals the difference
- 'Iron sharpeneth iron' - good friends make you MORE effective
- True friend gives 'hearty counsel' - speaks from the heart, changes your atmosphere
- Faithful wounds of a friend > deceitful kisses of an enemy - Prov 27:6

### Toxic Friendships to Avoid
- Avoid angry/furious people - their volatility will entangle you - Prov 22:24
- Avoid talebearers/whisperers - they will eventually betray your confidence
- 'If they talk to you about others, they will talk to others about you'
- Separate by CHOICE, not consequence - preventative maintenance

### Counsel & Advisors
- 'Where no counsel is, the people fall' - Prov 11:14
- Good advice must be DRAWN OUT like deep water from a well - Prov 20:5
- Advice is only as good as its source
- Never seek counsel from someone emotionally attached to the problem
- Seek counselors who have what you want / are where you want to be
- Constructive confrontation > flattery - Prov 28:23

## PART 8: WORK, DILIGENCE & SUCCESS PRINCIPLES

### Production vs. Pleasure (Prov 24:27)
- Production Quadrant = activities with long-term benefits (work, fitness, relationships, faith)
- Pleasure Quadrant = activities with only immediate enjoyment
- Invest MOST of the day in production
- 'The more time in production, the more you can enjoy pleasure later - now or later'

### Wealth Principles
- 'Giving creates growth' - honor God with firstfruits - Prov 3:9-10
- Contributor (gives) > Consumer (hoards) - true wealth comes from generosity - Prov 13:7
- Diligent hand makes rich; slack hand makes poor - Prov 10:4
- Avoid debt - borrower is servant to lender - Prov 22:7
- Save and conserve - wise people have treasure in their house - Prov 21:20
- Desire without knowledge = failure (lottery winners, athletes who go broke)
- Do not be hasty for riches - Prov 28:20

### Commitment to God (Prov 16:3, 3:5-6, 21:31)
- 'Commit thy works unto the LORD and thy thoughts shall be established'
- Trust in God entirely; do not lean on your own understanding - Prov 3:5-6
- Acknowledge God in ALL decisions
- All success is ultimately ALLOWED by God - win humbly
- The 'Trust Factor' - choose trust over understanding when you cannot see the full picture

## PART 9: PRIDE vs. HUMILITY

### Pride
- 'Pride goeth before destruction' - Prov 16:18
- EGO = Edging God Out
- Pride causes: contention to conflict to consequences (often irreparable)
- Pride destroys character; causes people to act 'low' even when they feel 'high'
- Hard-headed + hard-hearted = scorner - God backs away

### Humility
- Humility is the ANTIDOTE to pride
- Humility forgives, forbears, forfeits rights to fulfill responsibilities
- 'Before honor is humility' - Prov 18:12
- Humility seeks to be honorable, not honored
- God gives grace to the humble; scorns the scorner - Prov 3:34

## PART 10: GIVING & THE POOR

- God has a SPECIAL CONNECTION to the poor - mistreating them is reproaching God
- 'He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the LORD' - Prov 19:17
- 'He that giveth unto the poor shall not lack' - Prov 28:27
- Giving activates sowing and reaping; generosity creates prosperity
- 'Bountiful eye' - see yourself as blessed, naturally give to others - Prov 22:9
- Predatory practices on the poor are an abomination to God - Prov 22:16, 28:8
- The virtuous woman (Prov 31) STRETCHES HERSELF to help the poor - proactive generosity
- We are blessed to BE a blessing - this is a divine responsibility

## PART 11: PRACTICAL WISDOM PRINCIPLES

### Anger & Conflict
- Defer anger over most offenses - Prov 19:11
- 'It is an honour for a man to cease from strife' - Prov 20:3
- Avoiding conflict is wiser than winning conflict
- An offended brother = harder to win than a strong city - Prov 18:19

### Integrity
- 'Better is the poor that walketh in his integrity' - Prov 19:1
- Integrity = walk is congruent with talk; behavior backs up words
- 'Integrity equals influence'

### Reputation
- A good name > riches - Prov 22:1
- Even children are known by their doings - Prov 20:11
- Reputation is built continuously whether or not we are conscious of it

### Hasty Decisions
- 'He that hasteth with his feet sinneth' - Prov 19:2
- Rushed decisions = mistakes; 'Haste makes waste'
- If pressured to decide quickly, that is a red flag
- Desire without knowledge = failure; pair passion with preparation

### Knowledge Accumulation
- 'Wise men lay up knowledge' - Prov 10:14
- Save knowledge BEFORE you need it
- Seek knowledge on purpose; make daily deposits into your 'wisdom savings account'

### Adversity & Resilience
- 'If thou faint in the day of adversity, thy strength is small' - Prov 24:10
- Adversity is life's university - we graduate or repeat the grade
- 'A just man falleth seven times and riseth up again' - Prov 24:16
- The choice after falling: get up and move on, or stay down

### Every Blessing Has a Burden (Prov 14:4)
- 'Where no oxen are, the crib is clean: but much increase is by the strength of the ox'
- Do not complain about the mess (burden) created by your blessing
- 'Burden-conscious vs. Blessing-conscious'

6. POSITIVE SELF-ESTEEM - confident in her work; energetic - Prov 31:18
7. GENEROUS TO THE POOR - stretches herself proactively to help - Prov 31:20
8. ELEGANT & TASTEFUL - clothes herself and family well; dignified - Prov 31:21-22
9. SUPPORTIVE - enables husband to be known and respected publicly - Prov 31:23
10. STRONG, HONORABLE, FAITH-FILLED - optimistic about the future - Prov 31:25

### Key Warning from Wise Mother

- Look at: Appearance, Attitude, Attributes, Actions, Abilities
- Most important: AGREEMENT - spiritual, directional, role agreement
- 'Can two walk together except they be agreed?' (Amos 3:3)

## PART 16: ADDITIONAL NOTABLE PRINCIPLES

### The Alignment Principle
When a person has a clear vision and focuses their faith on it, the right people, resources, and opportunities 'align' - doors open, help appears. Treated as a spiritual law throughout the book.

### Sowing & Reaping (Divine Law)
What you sow, you will reap - in relationships, finances, work ethic, words. Presented as inescapable divine law. The fool reaps negative; the wise reap positive.

### The Tree of Life Metaphor (Prov 3:18)
Wisdom is a tree of life - continuously producing fruit. Foolishness is a tree of death. The recurring question: 'Which tree are you eating from?'

### The Two Paths
Solomon constantly presents TWO clear paths:
- Path of the Wise = blessed, peaceful, prosperous, honorable
- Path of the Fool = cursed, chaotic, poor, shameful
The consequences of each path can pass to the NEXT GENERATION.

### The Trust Factor (Prov 16:33)
God is Master even of seemingly random chance. When we cannot understand circumstances, TRUST is the answer. Our goal in difficult circumstances should be peace, not explanation.

### Forgiveness & Letting Go (Prov 20:22, 19:11)
- Do not seek revenge - let God handle it
- Deferring anger = glory; letting go = strength
- The wise let God reveal truth over time

### Gifts & Giftedness (Prov 18:16)
'A man's gift maketh room for him' - discover and develop your unique giftedness; it opens doors that position and title cannot. Stop trying to be a copy of someone else.

### The Production Principle (Prov 12:14)
Success comes from combining WORDS + WORK. Words without work = nothing. Work without strategic words = limited. The greatest leaders leverage both.

### Lessons from Creation (Prov 30:24-28)
Four small but wise creatures:
1. ANT - power of preparation
2. CONIE - power of adapting and improvising
3. LOCUST - power of teamwork (bands together for massive impact)
4. SPIDER - power of tenacity (builds back every time it is knocked down)
