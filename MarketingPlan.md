## BRAND FOUNDATION

### Core Promise
Solomon asked God for wisdom — and received wealth, honor, peace, and influence that the world had never seen. That is the entire thesis of this brand. Not "get tips." Not "feel better." **Seek wisdom first, and everything else follows.**

Every piece of marketing content, every email, every social post, every SEO page should carry this underneath it.

### Brand Voice
- **Tone:** A wise, loving father speaking to a son or daughter
- **Style:** Warm, direct, never preachy, practical, grounded in Proverbs
- **Method:** Cause-and-effect language — *when you do X, the natural result is Y*
- **Contrast:** Always presents two paths — the wise path and the fool's path
- **Promise:** Always points toward the better path; "it is not too late"
- **Credibility anchor:** Solomon was the most successful, wealthiest, wisest person in history — and he documented exactly how and why

### Primary Tagline
> **"Biblical Wisdom for Life's Real Moments"**

### Supporting Taglines / Campaign Hooks
- *"Solomon had an answer. Ask him."*
- *"Seek wisdom first. The rest follows."*
- *"3,000 years of wisdom. Applied to right now."*
- *"The most successful man in history left a blueprint. This is it."*

## TARGET AUDIENCES

### Primary: The Stuck Believer (Ages 28–55)
Christian adults who feel stuck in some area — work, relationships, finances, purpose, or their own habits. They read devotional content. They want practical application, not just inspiration. They are ready to buy the companion book if the app earns their trust.

**Where they are:** Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, church small groups, Christian podcasts
**What they search:** "What does the Bible say about [anxiety/money/success/relationships]"
**What they want:** Wisdom that works in the real world, not just the Sunday world

### Secondary: The Spiritually Curious Non-Churchgoer (Ages 24–45)
Not necessarily religious but open to ancient wisdom. Respects Proverbs as a practical guide to life. The app's "no fluff, no AI, just Proverbs" positioning resonates. Attracted by the success/wealth/leadership angle more than the devotional angle.

**Where they are:** TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, podcast apps
**What they search:** "Stoic wisdom for success," "ancient success principles," "how to build wealth"
**What they want:** Timeless, practical frameworks — not religiosity

### Tertiary: Multipliers (Pastors, Small Group Leaders, Christian Coaches)
People with built-in audiences who can recommend the app to 50–500 people at once. A pastor recommending the app to a congregation is worth more than 500 individual ads.

**Where they are:** Church networks, Christian coaching communities, ministry podcasts
**What they want:** A trusted tool they can confidently hand to people they are helping

## PILLAR 1 — WEEKLY WISDOM DROPS
### *Short-form video content: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts*

**Concept:** One Proverb per week applied to a modern situation. 30–60 seconds. Punchy, visual, shareable.

**The book's contrast device is the engine:** Every post uses the Wise vs. Fool framework — the most naturally engaging content structure in Proverbs.

### Post Formula
```
HOOK (3 sec): A relatable modern problem stated boldly
  → "You work hard but you're always broke. Solomon identified why."
  → "Most people handle anger wrong. Here's what the wisest man in history said."
  → "The reason you keep failing at the same thing over and over."

BRIDGE (10 sec): The Proverb that speaks to it
  → Text on screen + voiceover reading the verse

PRINCIPLE (30 sec): The wisdom from Success Secrets of Solomon
  → The wise person does X. The fool does Y. Here is the difference.

CTA (5 sec): "Ask Solomon" with link in bio / sticker
  → "What are YOU facing right now? Ask Solomon at the link."
```

## CONVERSION STRATEGY — FREE TO PRO

### The $29 Lifetime Offer — Positioning
The book teaches: *seek wisdom like treasure.* The conversion message leans into this.
- "You paid more than $29 for lunch. Lifetime access to the wisdom that built empires costs less."
- "Solomon asked for wisdom — not wealth. You are investing in the same thing he invested in."
- "One conversation with a good counselor costs $150/hour. Solomon's wisdom is $29 forever."

### Conversion Trigger Points
1. **After 2nd app search:** Gentle overlay — "Unlock full access for $29 lifetime"
2. **Book connection field:** Every wisdom response shows a book chapter reference — "Read more in *Success Secrets of Solomon* — Pro unlocks the full library"
3. **Email Day 8:** First hard Pro pitch after 7 days of free value
4. **Solomon Challenge Day 14:** Mid-challenge upgrade offer
5. **SEO page bottom:** "Want deeper wisdom? Upgrade to Pro"

### Free Value First (The Book's Principle: Giving Creates Growth)
- The best wisdom responses are always available free
- The Pro offer is an *upgrade*, never a paywall on the core experience
- Give generously; the overflow creates the purchase decision
